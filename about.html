About the organisation

Rooted in community.
Focused on possibility.

Skilling Development Initiative (SDI) is a community-based organisation legally registered to operate in Kamwenge District, Uganda, working for economic empowerment and social wellbeing.

Our story & context

Responding to the challenges communities face.

SDI is aware of the effects of illiteracy, poverty, unemployment and environmental degradation in the community. The organisation works to strengthen livelihoods and wellbeing through practical skills training and community-based development.

Its work includes vulnerable people such as orphans, child mothers, persons with disabilities and school dropouts, as well as men and women seeking better standards of living.

SDI TEAM & COMMUNITY
Our vision
Empowered citizens. Decent standards of living.

The future SDI is working towards.

Our mission

Improving household wellbeing.

To improve household income and welfare, food security, health, good governance, environmental protection and quality education to reduce suffering of men and women, youths and children.
