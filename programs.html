What we deliver

Skills for work.
Knowledge for life.

SDI combines practical vocational and technical training, agribusiness, ICT, climate-smart agriculture and community empowerment to support decent employment and sustainable livelihoods.

Our programme pathways

Learn practical skills. Build sustainable livelihoods.

SDI provides practical learning opportunities designed to equip vulnerable youth, women, child mothers, persons with disabilities and other community members with useful skills for life and work.

AGRIBUSINESS AND VEGETABLE PRODUCTION
Programme 01

Agribusiness

Practical agricultural training that helps community members develop food production skills, improve household food security and explore income-generating opportunities.

Available crops and vegetables include:

Sukumawiki, spinach, cucumber, Chinese cabbage, eggplants, spring onions, tomatoes, carrots, cayenne peppers, green pepper, white radishes, and other crops grown at the demonstration sites.

Demonstration sites:
SDI Optimum Land Utilization (OLU) Site, Nkoma
Ntenungi Outreach Demonstration Site
TAILORING AND FASHION
Programme 02

Tailoring and Fashion

Practical training in tailoring, fashion design and garment production to help trainees develop skills for employment and self-employment.

What trainees learn:

Taking body measurements, preparing patterns, fabric cutting, sewing, garment construction, fashion design and finishing.

Focus: Practical garment-making skills
Outcome: Opportunities for employment, self-employment and small businesses.
MOTORCYCLE REPAIR AND MECHANICS
Programme 03

Motorcycle Repair

Hands-on mechanical training designed to equip trainees with practical motorcycle maintenance and repair skills.

What trainees learn:

Basic motorcycle inspection, maintenance, identifying mechanical faults, repair practices, workshop safety and proper handling of tools.

Focus: Motorcycle maintenance and repair
Outcome: Skills for workshop employment or self-employment.
LEATHER DESIGN AND CRAFTSMANSHIP
Programme 04

Leather Design

Practical leatherwork and craft training where participants learn to make useful products using locally available materials.

What trainees learn:

Cutting, shaping, hammering, stitching and assembling colourful sandals and other leather products.

Training example: Sandal making
Outcome: Practical craft skills that can support income generation and household livelihoods.
Watch sandal-making training →
ICT AND DIGITAL SKILLS
Programme 05

ICT and Digital Skills

Practical digital skills training that helps participants use technology to communicate, learn, document their work and promote their businesses.

Compture & Smartphone photography training:

Guided by a facilitator, participants learn how to frame, focus and capture clear photos using their phones. They can use these skills to document their work, tell their stories and promote their businesses online.

Photo activity: Youths Learn Smartphone Photography
Location: SDI, Rwamwanja
Date: November 2026
GREEN TVET AND ENVIRONMENTAL SKILLS
Programme 06

Green TVET

Green Technical and Vocational Education and Training promotes practical skills that support environmental protection and sustainable livelihoods.

Programme focus:

Environmentally responsible practices, efficient use of available resources, sustainable production, tree planting and climate-conscious activities.

Focus: Green skills and environmental awareness
Outcome: Practical knowledge that supports sustainable communities.
