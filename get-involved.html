For prospective trainees

Interested in learning a practical skill?

SDI's programmes are designed to reach vulnerable people, including orphans, child mothers, persons with disabilities and school dropouts, as well as women and men seeking better livelihood opportunities.

  • 1.Contact SDI and explain the training area you are interested in.
  • 2.Ask about current or upcoming training cohorts and availability.
  • 3.Confirm eligibility, required documents, training location and dates directly with SDI.

Specific age limits, application deadlines, fees, required documents and cohort dates were not included in the supplied brief. Please confirm them with SDI before applying.
